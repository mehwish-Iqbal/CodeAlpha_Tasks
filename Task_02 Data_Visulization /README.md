## 📌 Introduction

This project focuses on transforming Olist Brazilian E-Commerce data into clear, meaningful, and business-focused visualizations.

The main goal is to identify sales trends, customer patterns, product performance, payment behavior, and delivery patterns using Python visualization libraries.

* Use appropriate visualizations to communicate findings clearly.

* Build a visualization project suitable for a professional portfolio.

## 🔧 Data Preparation

The datasets were previously cleaned and validated during the EDA task.

For this visualization task, the required preprocessing and dataset merging steps were performed again to prepare the data for visualization.

### Main preparation steps:

* Converted date columns to datetime format.
* Translated Portuguese product categories into English.
* Merged order items, products, orders, and customer data.
* Created the main `sales_data` dataset.
* Prepared aggregated datasets for individual visualizations.

**Final `sales_data` shape:** `112,650 × 16`

# 📈 Visualizations

## 1. Monthly Sales Revenue Trend

This visualization shows how product sales revenue changed over time.

**Insight:** Sales generally increased from early 2017 and reached strong revenue levels during late 2017 and early-to-mid 2018. November 2017 recorded the highest monthly revenue at approximately **$1.01 million**.

![Monthly Sales Revenue Trend](images/monthly_revenue.png)

---

## 2. Top 10 Product Categories by Revenue

This chart compares the revenue generated by the top-performing product categories.

**Insight:** Health & Beauty generated the highest revenue at approximately **$1.26 million**, followed by Watches & Gifts and Bed Bath Table.

![Top 10 Product Categories by Revenue](images/top_categories.png)

---

## 3. Top 10 States by Number of Customers

This visualization shows the states with the largest customer base.

**Insight:** São Paulo (SP) had the highest number of customers with **39,981**, followed by Rio de Janeiro (RJ) and Minas Gerais (MG).

![Top 10 States by Customers](images/customer_states.png)

---

## 4. Payment Method Distribution

This chart shows the number of payments made through each payment method.

**Insight:** Credit cards were the most frequently used payment method, with **76,795 transactions**, followed by boleto.

![Payment Method Distribution](images/payment_methods.png)

---

## 5. Orders by Day of the Week

This visualization compares the number of orders received on different days of the week.

**Insight:** Monday recorded the highest number of orders with **16,196**, while Saturday had the lowest with **10,887** orders.

![Orders by Day of Week](images/orders_by_day.png)

---

## 6. Revenue by Day of the Week

This chart compares total product sales revenue across different days of the week.

**Insight:** Monday generated the highest revenue at approximately **$2.62 million**, while Saturday generated the lowest at approximately **$1.77 million**.

![Revenue by Day of Week](images/revenue_by_day.png)

---

## 7. Product Price vs Freight Value

This scatter plot examines the relationship between product price and freight value.

**Insight:** Freight charges vary across products and categories, while higher-priced products do not always have proportionally higher freight costs. This highlights the importance of considering shipping costs separately from product pricing.

![Product Price vs Freight Value](images/price_vs_freight.png)

---

## 8. Top 10 Product Categories by Average Delivery Difference

This visualization compares the average difference between actual and estimated delivery dates across product categories.

**Insight:** The visualization highlights differences in delivery performance across categories and can help identify categories that may require closer delivery monitoring.

![Average Delivery Difference](images/delivery_difference.png)

# 💡 Business Insights

The visual analysis highlighted several important patterns:

* Sales showed an overall upward trend across the main period of the dataset.
* Health & Beauty was the highest-revenue product category.
* São Paulo had the largest customer base.
* Credit cards were the most commonly used payment method.
* Monday had both the highest order volume and highest revenue.
* Saturday recorded the lowest revenue and order volume.
* Delivery performance varied across product categories.
* Product price and freight value showed varying relationships across products.

---

# 📖 Business Story

The visualization analysis shows that Olist's sales activity was concentrated in specific product categories and customer regions. Health & Beauty generated the highest revenue, while São Paulo represented the largest customer market.

Payment behavior was strongly centered around credit cards. Weekly analysis showed higher sales activity on Monday and lower activity on Saturday.

Delivery analysis also revealed differences between product categories, suggesting that delivery performance should be monitored alongside sales performance.

---

# 🎯 Business Decisions

Based on the visual findings, businesses can:

* Focus on high-performing product categories.
* Monitor major customer regions for growth opportunities.
* Consider high-demand days when planning promotions.
* Maintain popular payment options such as credit cards.
* Monitor delivery performance across product categories.
* Use sales and delivery trends to improve operational planning.
